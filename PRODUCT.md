# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** looking at Pallab for internships or junior full-stack / AI/ML roles. They need a fast read of skills and shipped work, then a clear path to the resume or the "Hire me" email.
- **Fellow developers** arriving from GitHub, X or LinkedIn. They explore projects, writing and the playful parts, and may follow or reach out.

## Product Purpose

Pallab Das's personal site (pallabdas.me): who he is, what he has built, what he is learning, and how to contact or hire him. Success is a recruiter reaching the resume or email in seconds, and a developer remembering the site afterwards.

## Positioning

A Software Engineering student at VIT Vellore who builds full-stack and AI/ML things in public: a live Backpack-style crypto exchange (OpenExchange), an Excalidraw-style whiteboard, a Zapier-style automation platform. Personal handle PDGamerSG; daily MonkeyType typist (259-day streak, 120 wpm best at time of writing).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a resume link, GitHub profile, or social bio, mostly on desktop (recruiters) and phones (social traffic). Pages: Home, Writing, Projects, About, external Resume PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, deployed as a static-first site. Live data: GitHub contributions and MonkeyType stats fetched at build/revalidate time.
- Desktop navbar motion stays limited to the active-link squiggle (user request, 2026-10-04). On phones the nav is a bottom keycap dock, and that one may animate (user request, 2026-10-05).
- Elements the user requires on the homepage: the interactive dot-matrix name, the MonkeyType-style typed tagline, the Space Snake game (bottom of page), and the Claude quote.
- Internship at Biz Trade Mart ran May 2026 to Sep 2026 and has ended.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Pallab Das", handle "PDGamerSG", casual first-person voice with light humour ("Touching grass", "I like it tho").
- Socials: GitHub, LinkedIn, X (use the X mark, not the Twitter bird), Instagram.

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile photo `public/pfp.jpg`; project screenshots in `public/projects/`.
- Resume `public/Pallab-Das-Resume.pdf`.
- Live stats from GitHub and MonkeyType APIs.
- No testimonials, clients or metrics beyond these; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't list: work and habits should be seen, not described in bullet points.
2. Personality is part of the pitch: the gamer handle, the typing habit and the space snake are what make the site his.
3. The recruiter path is never more than one glance away.
4. Everything shown is true and current.
